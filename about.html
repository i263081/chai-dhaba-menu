About Our Dhaba

Welcome to Chai Corner Dhaba! We serve authentic, freshly brewed Karak Chai along with traditional parathas and savory snacks.

Established with a love for traditional flavors, our recipe combines rich milk, fresh spices, and premium tea leaves for the perfect cup every single time.

Why Choose Us?
